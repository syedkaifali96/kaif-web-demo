ABOUT THE STUDIO

Small by design.
Thoughtful by nature.

Forma is an illustrative independent studio built around a simple idea: the best creative work makes things clearer.

01 / THE WAY WE THINK

CLARITY BEFORE COMPLEXITY

Good questions.
Better decisions.

The studio concept brings strategy, identity and digital design into one connected process. Each choice should support the idea behind the business, from a first sketch to the final page.

Our proposed way of working is collaborative and direct: listen carefully, show the thinking, make space for feedback and keep the outcome easy to use.

A FEW THINGS TO DESIGN BY

01

Make it clear.

People should understand what you do and why it matters without having to work for it.

02

Make it distinctive.

The visual language should come from the character of the business, rather than the trend of the moment.

03

Make it useful.

Design has a job to do. Every element should help someone learn, decide or take the next step.

A CONSIDERED PROCESS

Room to explore.
A clear way forward.

Each phase has a purpose, a review point and an agreed next step.

  1. 01

    Listen & understand

    Agree on the audience, the business goals, the scope and what a successful outcome looks like.

  2. 02

    Explore & refine

    Present a focused creative direction, explain the thinking and refine it with your feedback.

  3. 03

    Build & hand over

    Develop the agreed design, test the details and provide the files and guidance needed to maintain it.
