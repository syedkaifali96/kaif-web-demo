BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
details.

What should I send first?+

A short brief, the pages you need, your content and logo, reference websites, your deadline and estimated budget. Use images you have permission to publish.

How is the price decided?+

We discuss the page count, features, content readiness and revisions first. A budget selected in the form is a starting point for a conversation, rather than a fixed quote.

Do you use AI tools?+

Yes. My development process is AI-assisted. I review the code and check the result, and I’m open about being a student still building experience.

Can I update the website later?+

A static website includes its source files and editing notes. If you need a content management system, online payments or a complex backend, mention that in the brief so we can assess the scope first.
