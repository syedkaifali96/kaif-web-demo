BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few good
questions.

Can we start with just a website?+

Yes. An existing logo, brand guide and approved content can be the starting point. The initial scope would identify the required pages, features and handover needs.

How is the price decided?+

A quote follows the brief. Page count, integrations, content readiness, revisions and deployment all affect the scope. The price and milestones would be agreed before paid work begins.

What would we need to provide?+

Your logo and brand guide, approved copy, licensed images, any reference sites and the required hosting access. Placeholder content can be used during the initial layout review.

Will the website be easy to update?+

The editing approach would be agreed at the start. A simple static build includes an editing guide; a visual content editor can be scoped separately if it is needed.
